# Controle art (Gemini)

Light watercolour on paper, to match the paper-and-ink theme. Big scenery only as painted art, never drawn SVG.
Generate one image per message in one Gemini chat, never a contact sheet: sheet crops come out around 240px and look soft on a retina screen. Paste the style block after every subject. After the first, start with "Same style as the previous image:". Save each at full size (at least 1024px on the short side) with the file name below.

## Style block
Style: delicate ink line drawing with soft watercolour washes on warm cream paper, high fantasy, calm and airy, lots of empty paper around the subject, muted palette of warm ink brown, soft gold and a touch of burnt orange at dusk, gentle light from the right. No text, no letters, no logos, no frame, no border, no people in the foreground. Ward images: portrait 4:5. Night images: square 1:1.

## Placement
- valley.jpg: full-width picture on the welcome screen.
- Ward images: framed beside each lesson's title and facts (170px), and in that ward's flyout header.
- Night images: framed beside the scouts' briefing (220px).
- Files live in img/ as .jpg. A new image with the same name drops in; update its size in ARTD.
