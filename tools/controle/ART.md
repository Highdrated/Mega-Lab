# Controle art (Gemini)

Light watercolour on paper, to match the paper-and-ink theme. Big scenery only as painted art, never drawn SVG.
Generate all images in one Gemini chat. Paste the style block after every subject. After the first, start with "Same style as the previous image:".

## Style block
Style: delicate ink line drawing with soft watercolour washes on warm cream paper, high fantasy, calm and airy, lots of empty paper around the subject, muted palette of warm ink brown, soft gold and a touch of burnt orange at dusk, gentle light from the right. No text, no letters, no logos, no frame, no border, no people in the foreground. Wide 16:9.

## Placement
- Ward images: banner at the top of that ward's lessons, and at the top of its flyout.
- Night images: top of the scouts' briefing.
