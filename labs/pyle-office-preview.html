The clerk needs a menu board for the dining car. Print the price of cake, add dinner at 30, then list every dish with its price.

When there is no coffee tonight, say so.
menu_board.pyoffice car · Pyle
"""Menu board for the dining car.""" menu = {"tea": 4, "soup": 9, "cake": 12} print("Cake costs", menu["cake"]) menu["dinner"] = 30 for dish, price in menu.items(): print(f"{dish}: {price} fr") if "coffee" not in menu: print("No coffee tonight")▍
